Hisab - হিসাব ("Hisab", "we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance and daily expense management application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

In short: Your financial records are intended to remain on your device. Hisab does not require an account or sign-in to record your financial information.

1. Information You Enter

You may choose to enter information such as your name, mobile number, occupation, email address, address, profile information, income, expenses, categories, loan records, receivable records, descriptions, dates, amounts, payment information, reminders, and photos or voucher information.

This information is used to provide transaction tracking, summaries, reports, loan and receivable management, reminders, and backup/restore features.

2. How Your Financial Data Is Stored

Hisab stores its app data locally on your device using local app/browser storage. The app is designed so that your financial records are not uploaded to a Hisab-operated server.

The app provides local export and backup options. When you export a TXT or JSON backup, the file is created on your device. If you choose to share, move, upload, or otherwise send that backup file using another service, that service's privacy policy will apply.

3. Backup and Restore

Hisab can create TXT and JSON backup files and restore data from a compatible JSON backup file. Backup files may contain financial records and profile information. You are responsible for keeping exported backup files secure.

4. App Lock and PIN

Hisab provides an optional app-lock feature with a four-digit PIN. The PIN is stored locally as part of the app's local data and is not intentionally transmitted to a Hisab server.

If you use a device-level biometric/security feature provided by Android or the app wrapper, biometric processing is handled by the relevant platform security system. Hisab does not receive your fingerprint image or biometric template.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app may display advertisements using Google AdMob or related Google advertising technology. Advertising providers may collect or process certain technical information, such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, app interactions, and information needed to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to the provider's policies and the choices available on your device.

Hisab does not intentionally send your financial transaction records, loan details, receivable details, or profile data to an advertising provider for advertising purposes.

Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Third-Party Web Resources

Depending on how Hisab is packaged and used, the app may load third-party web resources such as fonts or web libraries. Those providers may receive standard technical information such as an IP address when your device requests their resources. Their own privacy policies govern such processing.

7. Notifications, Vibration and Device Features

Hisab may use device features such as notifications, vibration, sound, local storage, camera/photo selection, printing, and file sharing when you use the related features. These features are used to provide the functionality you request.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal financial information. Hisab is designed not to share your locally stored financial records with third parties through a Hisab-operated backend.

Information may nevertheless be processed by third-party services when you independently choose to use those services, for example when you view advertisements or share a backup file through another application.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete stored app data using the app's available delete or reset options. You can also remove the app from your device. Exported backup files are separate files and must be deleted separately from your device or from any service where you chose to store or share them.

10. Children's Privacy
